### The deal in one line

**20% to you** for each sale · **20% to me** for each build · **the company keeps the rest**.

### Ownership

- **Day one:** you own **5%**
- **Day 366:** you get another **5%** (10% total)

- **End of year 3:** another **5%** (20% total)

If you leave before a chunk has earned in, that chunk goes back to the company.

### How each new customer pays out

- Published pricing only — **no setup / onboarding fees**
- Every new customer signs a **12-month** contract
- On that customer’s **first year** of payments:
  - **You: 20%**
  - **Me: 20%** (for building / delivering the site)
  - **Company: 60%** (before operating costs)
- After their first year, your commission on that customer **stops**. Renewals help you as an **owner** through dividends instead.

### Hit quota, then get paid double on extras

Every year:

- Clients **1–12:** you earn **20%**
- Clients **13 and up:** you earn **40%** (double commission)
- My build cut stays **20%** on every client, including the extras

If you rip past 12, I’m happy to double your take on those deals.

### Dividends

After commissions, build pay, and operating costs, leftover company profit is paid out as **dividends by ownership %**. We can do that yearly or quarterly — we’ll lock that in together.
